## Introduction

In November of 2008, voters faced a choice: Barack Obama, a senator from Illinois, and his running mate, Joe Biden, a long-time senator from Delaware; or John McCain, Arizona senator and decorated war veteran, and his running mate, Sarah Palin, the governor of Alaska. What was on the minds of Americans as they cast their ballots in the historic election? This clip from WQED’s OnQ features local Pittsburgh voters sharing their thoughts on the importance of the election, what issues they wanted the winner to address first, and who they expected to be the next president of the United States. Many respondents were excited to participate in the voting process, and cited the economy (which was in the middle of what would become known as the Great Recession) and the conflict in Afghanistan as top of mind issues.
