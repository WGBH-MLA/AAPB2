## Introduction

While Obama was becoming increasingly popular in the Democratic Party as a historic candidate and coalition builder, he faced staunch opposition from the right — not only questioning his politics, but also his citizenship. From AM talk radio to cable news to corners of the internet, conservative media outlets falsely alleged that Obama was not born in the United States and thus ineligible for the presidency. Despite being clearly debunked (Obama released his birth certificate proving he was born in Hawaii), these allegations continued even after his second term. The historians in this clip explain the birther conspiracy, how it tied into the discomfort and fear that many white Americans had about the first Black president, and its historical roots in discourses of race, ethnicity, othering, and white supremacy.
