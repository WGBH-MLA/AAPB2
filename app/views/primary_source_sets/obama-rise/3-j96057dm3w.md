## Introduction

In 2004, the Democratic National Convention Committee selected Obama to deliver the keynote address in support of John Kerry’s nomination for the party’s presidential candidate. At the time, he was an Illinois state senator and a relative newcomer to the national political stage, but his speech was a massive success. Utilizing a number of rhetorical devices and a rousing delivery style, Obama’s keynote address brought the arena crowd to its feet and left live broadcast commentators nearly speechless, except to remark that what we just witnessed was “a bit of history.”
