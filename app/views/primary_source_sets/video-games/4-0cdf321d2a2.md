## Introduction

In addition to concerns about truancy, juvenile delinquency, and predatory gambling practices,  the rise of video games in the 1980s also spurred debates about their potential impact on cognitive development. This excerpt from Chapel Hill, North Carolina’s WUNC features two sociology professors from the University of North Carolina discussing both the benefits and drawbacks of this new technology. On the one hand, they argue, video games may help strengthen social ties as friend groups gather to play together or share a common interest in playing. On the other hand, spending too much time with video games and other machines may crowd out other activities like reading, writing, and daydreaming that are crucial for cognitive development, they claim.
