## Introduction

Video games in the 1980s shifted the ways people interacted with machines and launched new norms of mediated communication. They also influenced how some early technology workers thought about the importance of graphics and interactive navigation — not just for games, but for machines more broadly. This excerpt from WGBH's series, The Machine That Changed the World, features an interview with information technology pioneer Ted Nelson. In it, he contends that the video game Pac-Man shaped how some early tech workers thought about design interfaces with computers, contributing to a broader shift from command-based interfaces like MS-DOS to the graphical user interfaces we know today, like Windows and Apple's iOS.
