## Introduction

The technological advances in video games also engendered advances in how games were imagined, written, and designed. This clip from a 1983 episode of KQED San Francisco’s program, *Bits, Bytes, and Buzzwords*, features an interview with two innovators in game design conversing about the ways creating interactive video games is akin to creating a small world for people to explore. The idea of video game development as “world building” is more commonplace today, but this interview offers an important precedent to that way of engaging with the medium.
