## Introduction

While we often think of video games in terms of computers and consoles, early video games in fact emerged alongside pinball machines within the world of coin-operated amusements – games where players would insert a coin to play to move metal balls around obstacles using flippers. These games became popular beginning in the early 1930s at commercial locations known as entertainment arcades, which featured coin-operated machines like pinball machines, shooting games, and fortune tellers. This clip from a 1980 Christian Science Monitor radio news segment covers the shift to video games at local arcades, highlighting the success of Space Invaders, developed by Taito in Japan and licensed/distributed in the U.S. by Midway Manufacturing in 1978.
