## Introduction

Some environmentalists argued that the incremental reform advanced by some climate advocates would never fix the problem of climate change. In this interview on To The Best Of Our Knowledge on Wisconsin Public Radio, author and activist Naomi Klein argues we could not reverse climate change “within the confines of our current political reality.” Instead, she argues that we need a “profound ideological shift” towards collectivism, increased taxation to fund the public institutions, efforts to reimagine transportation and redesign cities, and  a campaign “taking on consumer capitalism.” For Klein, the problem of climate change was so massive—and the cause of climate change so endemic to the logic of capitalism—that the solution could only be found in a extensive overhaul of our political and economic system.
