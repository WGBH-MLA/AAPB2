## Introduction

Stuart Brand, the environmentalist interviewed on this episode of To The Best Of Our Knowledge on Wisconsin Public Radio, describes himself as an “eco-pragmatist.” Recognizing the difficulty of getting humans to make the necessary sacrifices to significantly reduce carbon emissions, he argues that the most promising solutions to climate change came from embracing technological fixes—even ones that other environmentalists have traditionally criticized. In this interview, Brand makes the case for nuclear power, which had been fiercely opposed by environmentalists whose consciousness was shaped by disasters at nuclear power facilities at Three Mile Island, Chernobyl, and Fukushima. However, Brand argues that nuclear power plants are essential because they produce massive amounts of carbon-free power. Brand also advocates for the development of geoengineering strategies that would artificially alter the atmosphere and thus cool global temperatures—a Promethean approach that alarms many. But in Brand’s view, with our “back[s] against the wall,” humanity must pursue solutions that make us uncomfortable.
