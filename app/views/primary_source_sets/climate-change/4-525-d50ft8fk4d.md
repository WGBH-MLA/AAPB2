## Introduction

While climate change is often associated with rising sea levels and coastal flooding, [warming temperatures have also contributed to increasing wildfires](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/explore/wildfires-and-climate-change/). As California has gotten warmer and precipitation has declined, hot winds and dry vegetation have increased the frequency and intensity of these fires. In this 2017 PBS NewsHour segment, two climate scientists from Lamont Doherty Climate Observatory explain the science behind rising wildfire risk in California.
