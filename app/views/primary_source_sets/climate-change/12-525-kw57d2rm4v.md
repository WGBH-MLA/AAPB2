## Introduction

Despite the efforts of many countries to reduce greenhouse emissions, as evidenced by the global agreements at Kyoto and Paris, the threat of climate change continued and intensified. As referenced in this excerpt from the PBS Newshour, a 2019 United Nations report found that the previous five years had been the five warmest in human history, and global temperatures would continue to rise—bringing droughts, storms, and crop failures—unless countries dramatically increased their mitigation efforts. In this clip, reporter William Brangham describes events at the 2019 U.N. Climate Action Summit, including mass protests by climate activists. The report includes a particularly famous moment in which Swedish teen activist Greta Thunberg admonished delegates and expressed the frustration of many in the climate justice movement: “We are in the beginning of a mass extinction, and all you can talk about is money and fairy tales of eternal economic growth. How dare you!”
