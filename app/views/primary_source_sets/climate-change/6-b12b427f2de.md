## Introduction

While economists often supported carbon taxes as useful tools to encourage a shift away from carbon production, politicians have been reluctant to enact policies that would raise prices for consumers. The Inflation Reduction Act (IRA), passed with only Democratic support and signed by President Biden in 2022, tried a different strategy by using a carrot rather than a stick. As described in this PBS NewsHour interview with political strategist Paul Bledsoe of the Progressive Policy Institute, the IRA gave tax breaks to companies who invested in low-carbon energy production technology and consumers who purchased lower emission products like electric cars.
